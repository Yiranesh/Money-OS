// WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES

Three numbers that replace forty spreadsheet columns.

Money OS translates messy bank statements into three plain numbers so you never have to wonder if your card will decline before payday.

TICKET 01

Tracks every ringgit

Log your pocket allowance, PTPTN payout, or salary without tedious categories. Add an expense in three seconds right from your phone.

TICKET 02

Shows what is left

After rent and fixed commitments are set aside, you get one crystal-clear figure: your safe-to-spend allowance for meals and hangouts.

TICKET 03

Builds savings

Build a real emergency buffer starting with RM 50. Watch your reserve grow ringgit by ringgit without sacrificing your university social life.

// WHO IT HELPS

Built specifically for real life stages in Malaysia.

No corporate financial jargon, no predatory credit card promotions. Just calm financial clarity when you need it most.

01/ University students

For campus students balancing PTPTN, JPA, hostel rent, and mamak nights with friends without running dry before finals week.

02/ First-time earners

For first-time earners untangling net take-home pay after EPF, SOCSO, and EIS, while managing your first rental in KL or Penang.

03/ Anyone starting to save

For anyone starting completely from scratch with just RM 50 a month and zero previous spreadsheet or finance experience.
